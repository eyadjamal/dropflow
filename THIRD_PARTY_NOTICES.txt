DropFlow 3.4.0 — third-party components

DropFlow source is licensed under AGPL-3.0; see LICENSE.
The app uses Python, Tcl/Tk, Pillow, PyMuPDF, pystray, tkinterdnd2,
python-docx, python-pptx, openpyxl, reportlab, pytesseract,
arabic-reshaper, python-bidi and imageio-ffmpeg, with their dependencies.
Linux X11 shortcuts optionally use pynput and its dependencies.
PyInstaller bundles the platform runtime and packages. Distribution must
retain the applicable component notices and license terms from those packages.
Refer to the included package metadata and upstream projects for their full terms.

HEIC/HEIF image support and the pillow-heif image-codec dependency have been
removed from this build. FFmpeg video/audio functionality is unchanged; its
codec set and licensing are separate from image-format support.

External tools such as LibreOffice, Tesseract, 7-Zip/UnRAR and Microsoft Office
are not bundled by this build and retain their own terms.
